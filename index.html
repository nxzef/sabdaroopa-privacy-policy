Privacy Policy for Sabdaroopa

Last Updated: June 25, 2026
Effective Date: June 25, 2026

Overview

Sabdaroopa ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information in our Sanskrit declension table application.

Sabdaroopa does not collect personal information directly. However, the App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, AdMob collects certain information from your device, including device identifiers such as the Advertising ID. This policy explains what is collected and how it is used.

Information Collected Through Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process the following data from your device to deliver, personalize, and measure ads:

  • Device or other identifiers — such as the Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address
  • Device information — such as device model, operating system version, and language
  • Ad interaction data — such as ad views, taps, and similar engagement signals

This data is transmitted off your device to Google and is used for advertising, marketing, and analytics purposes (including measuring ad performance and limiting the number of times you see a particular ad). We, as the developer, do not receive your Advertising ID or personally identifiable information from this process.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Managing Ad Personalization

You can control or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads

Opting out does not remove ads, but it limits the use of your Advertising ID for personalization.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data only on your device (never transmitted to us):

  1. Favorite Words
    • Words you mark as favorites
    • Timestamp when favorited
    • Stored in a local database
  2. Word Visit Statistics
    • Number of times you view each word
    • Last visited timestamp
    • Used to show "recently viewed" suggestions
  3. App Preferences
    • Theme selection (Light/Dark/System)
    • Font size preferences
    • Vibration settings
    • Language preferences
    • Quiz defaults (mode and number of questions)

This locally stored data:

  • Stays on your device only
  • Is not transmitted to us or any server we control
  • Is not shared with any third party
  • Can be completely deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling the app

Internet Access

The App requires internet access. While the Sanskrit content itself works offline, an internet connection is used to load advertisements through Google AdMob. If no connection is available, the educational features of the App continue to function, but ads may not load.

App Permissions

Permissions We Use:

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

  • Purpose: Required to load advertisements via Google AdMob and to detect network availability

VIBRATE

  • Purpose: Provides haptic feedback when you interact with the App
  • Optional: Can be disabled in app settings

AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID)

  • Purpose: Allows Google AdMob to access the Advertising ID to serve and measure ads

Permissions We DO NOT Use:

  • Location access (GPS / precise location)
  • Camera access
  • Microphone access
  • Contacts access
  • Phone state access
  • Storage access (beyond the App's own data)

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Review — used to optionally prompt for an app rating. This is provided by Google Play services.

We do not use separate analytics services, crash reporting tools, social media integrations, or payment processors.

Data Security

  • We do not operate any server that stores your data.
  • Local data is stored using Android's Room database with standard platform security practices.
  • Data transmitted by Google AdMob is handled under Google's security and privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Sabdaroopa is intended as a general-audience educational application and is not directed to children under the age of 13. Because the App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, it may collect device identifiers as described above and is therefore not designed specifically for use by young children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us at the email below.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally Stored Data

To delete the data the App stores on your device:

Option 1: Clear App Data

  1. Go to your device Settings

Option 2: Uninstall the App

  1. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data from your device.

Advertising Data

Data collected by Google AdMob is retained and deleted according to Google's data retention practices. You can reset your Advertising ID at any time through your device settings as described above.
